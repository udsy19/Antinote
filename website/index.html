The first word decides what a note does.

Every note starts as plain text. Put one keyword on the first line and it becomes a checklist, a calculator or a timer. Delete the word and it’s plain text again.

Two colons, and a line runs a command.

Type :: anywhere to pick from the built-in extensions, then press Return. They’re small JavaScript files you can read, change, or install from GitHub.

::today
October 17, 2026
::today(7, eu)
24/10/2026
::workday(3)
October 21, 2026
::tip(120, 20, 4)
Tip: 24.00 · Total: 144.00 · Each: 36.00
::pick(pizza, sushi, tacos)
sushi
::sort · ::dedupe · ::trim

::format · ::replace_regex
Pretty-print JSON, find and replace with a pattern

Small app, deep pockets.

No folders and no titles to think up. Notes pile into a deck, newest on top, and everything else stays out of the way until you need it.

The deck
Swipe with two fingers or press ⌘[ ⌘]. Swipe past the newest note for a fresh one; empty notes tidy themselves away.
Nine slots
Pin the notes you keep coming back to on ⌘1…9. Open one beside the deck in split view.
The Void
Deleted notes drift here for 30 days, or as long as you choose, before they’re gone. Daily backups too.
Locked notes
Lock a note behind Touch ID or your password. It locks again a few minutes after you leave it.
Export
Send a note to Apple Notes, Obsidian or Bear, or save it as Markdown, text or PDF.
Encrypted sync
Optional. Notes are encrypted on your Mac, then synced through your own iCloud Drive.
Palette & search
⌘K runs any command, ⇧⌘F searches every note, archived ones included.
Themes & paper
Built-in light and dark themes or your own, lined, dotted or grid paper, and a translucent window.
Where it lives
Dock, menu bar, a drop-down under the menu bar, or nowhere but the hotkey.
Vim mode
Normal, Insert and Visual modes with counts, motions and operators.
Text from images
Drop a screenshot onto a note and its text is typed in for you, recognised on-device.
supernotes://
Create, append to and open notes from Shortcuts, Raycast, Alfred or the shell.

Your notes stay on your Mac.

Super Notes is a local app. There’s no account, no analytics and no server. Your notes never leave your Mac unless you turn on a feature that needs the network, and every one of those has its own switch.

  • Notes live in one SQLite file in your Library folder.
  • Sync encrypts every note on your Mac before it reaches iCloud Drive.
  • Extensions reach only the URLs they declare, with API keys you grant them.
  • Currency rates are the only switch that starts on. It downloads today’s rates and sends nothing of yours.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Super Notes is MIT-licensed open source. There’s no account, no subscription and no pro tier.

Why does macOS say Super Notes can’t be opened?

Super Notes isn’t notarized by Apple. The first time you open a copy downloaded in your browser, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once, and the one-line installer doesn’t need it.

Does anything go online?

Only what you allow in Settings → Privacy. Currency rates start on so currency math works right away; extension updates, extension APIs and sync start off. Turn Everything Off makes Super Notes fully offline.

How do I use it on two Macs?

Turn on Sync notes on both. Notes are encrypted on your Mac and synced through your own iCloud Drive. The key travels in iCloud Keychain, or you can copy it across by hand.

Can I use an AI model?

The bundled llm extension adds ::ask, ::summarize, ::rewrite and ::translate. Bring your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini or OpenRouter key, or point it at a local Ollama model. It does nothing until you switch on Extension APIs.

Can I get my notes out?

Any time. Export a note to Apple Notes, Obsidian, Bear, Markdown, text or PDF, or export every note at once as Markdown files plus a JSON file that another Mac can import.

What do I need?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. Run the install command again to update, or add -s -- --uninstall after bash to remove Super Notes.
